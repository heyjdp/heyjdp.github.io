I'm Dr David "Jas" Powell — engineering manager, hands-on engineer, and team builder based in Larnaca, Cyprus. I have worked across enterprise companies and fast-moving startups, had two successful exits, and I've spent my career straddling the line between leadership and the terminal.

I have a PhD in High Performance Computing and Semiconductor Engineering.

I've taken on remote Management, Leadership, CTO and Head of Engineering roles with startup teams across San Francisco, Milan, Tel-Aviv and Cyprus. Four CTO roles, two Head of Engineering roles, and plenty of times as an individual contributor. The companies that I have worked with have had broad focus across FinTech, Automotive, Cybersecurity, Maritime and Virtualisation.

## What I Do

I build engineering teams and I build software for high-performance distributed system. Usually at the same time.

My technical depth runs through **C/C++**, **Python**, **PostgreSQL**, and **AWS** — from bare-metal embedded systems and cryptography to cloud-scale backends and network architecture. I'm equally comfortable in a C-suite strategy conversation and a two-hour debugging session at 9pm.

I bring experience in **cybersecurity**, **OS hardening**, **cloud systems architecture**, **networking protocol design**, and **embedded OS customisation** (Yocto, Debian, Raspberry Pi). The kind of work where you need to care about every byte.
